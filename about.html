Built by people who've run IT in-house.

Geoweb started in 2012 as a two-person shop fixing servers after hours. We're larger now, but every engineer here has sat on the other side of the ticket queue.

Our story

Geoweb was founded by two systems administrators who kept getting asked, by friends running small companies, to "just take a look" at their networks. What started as favors became a formal practice once the favors stopped fitting into evenings and weekends.

Fourteen years later, the company still runs on that same instinct: understand what's actually broken before proposing anything, and leave every client's systems better documented than we found them.

Today Geoweb supports manufacturing firms, healthcare practices, logistics companies and retailers — organizations where IT isn't the product, but where it quietly determines whether the product ships on time.

We stay intentionally mid-sized. Big enough to cover 24/7 support and a bench of specialists; small enough that clients get engineers, not account managers reading from a script.

What guides the work

A few principles we hold engineers to, regardless of contract size.

Diagnose before you prescribe

No recommendation goes out until we can explain, in plain terms, what problem it solves.

Leave it documented

Passwords, diagrams and decisions are handed over in a form your next hire can actually use.

Say the hard thing early

If a budget or timeline won't work, we say so in the first meeting, not the third invoice.
