Six services, one accountable team.

Each service below can be scoped as a one-off project or folded into an ongoing managed contract. Pricing is quoted after the initial audit, once we know what's actually involved.

01

Cloud & infrastructure

We plan and execute migrations to AWS, Azure or hybrid setups, then keep the environment tuned so costs don't creep up quietly. This covers provisioning, backup strategy, and disaster-recovery testing on a schedule you actually get reminded about.

Cloud migration Backup & recovery Cost optimization
02

Cybersecurity

Risk assessments start with your actual attack surface, not a generic checklist. From there we implement endpoint protection, email filtering and access controls, and run tabletop exercises so your team knows the incident-response plan before they need it.

Risk assessment Endpoint protection Incident response
03

24/7 help desk

A support line staffed around the clock, with tickets logged, tracked and searchable — not lost in an inbox. Response-time SLAs are written into the contract, and monthly reports show exactly what came in and how it was resolved.

Ticketing SLA-backed support On-site & remote
04

Business systems integration

ERP, CRM and internal tools rarely talk to each other out of the box. We build the integrations that let data move between systems automatically, so staff stop re-entering the same customer record three times a week.

ERP / CRM API integration Workflow automation
05

IT strategy & roadmapping

A yearly technology plan tied to your actual budget cycle, covering hardware refresh timing, software licensing renewals, and where upcoming growth will strain current systems first.

Roadmapping Budget planning Vendor management
06

Staff training

Short, practical sessions — phishing recognition, password hygiene, and walkthroughs of any new system we roll out — scheduled around shift patterns instead of assuming everyone can attend a Tuesday morning webinar.

Security awareness Onboarding Tool adoption
